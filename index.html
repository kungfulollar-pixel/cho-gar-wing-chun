Why Cho Gar Wing Chun

A Rare and Authentic Wing Chun Tradition

Cho Gar Wing Chun is a distinct, family-preserved branch of Wing Chun Kung Fu — practical, direct, and taught with uncompromising authenticity.

🥋

Authentic Family Lineage

Learn directly from a member of the Cho family — a rare, unbroken line of transmission rooted in tradition, not a franchise.

A Living Tradition

Cho Gar Wing Chun preserves techniques and training methods passed down for generations, long unknown outside China.

🤝

Chi Sau — Sticky Hands

Develop reflexes, sensitivity and structure through Wing Chun's signature Chi Sau practice.
